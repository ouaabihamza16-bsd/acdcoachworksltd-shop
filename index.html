About us

A small, owner-run vehicle repair company

ACD COACHWORKS LTD carries out motor vehicle maintenance and repair. "Coachwork" is the traditional word for a vehicle's body, and bodywork is where our name comes from: repairing damaged panels, putting paintwork right and keeping vehicles in good working order.

The company was incorporated on 3 October 2020 and is registered in England and Wales under company number 12925134. It is owner-run, with a single director, Andrew Craig Darby, so you deal directly with the person who runs the business.

Our registered office, used for official correspondence, is in Cambridgeshire. You'll find the full address in the contact section.

What we do

Services

Our work covers the body, the finish and the general mechanical upkeep of motor vehicles.

Body repairs

Repairs to damaged panels and bodywork after everyday knocks, scrapes and minor accidents. We look at the damage, explain what needs doing and repair the affected areas so the body is straight and sound again.

  • Damaged panels and bodywork
  • Dents and scratches
  • Repairs after minor accidents

Paint and refinishing

Paintwork repairs and refinishing to restore your vehicle's finish. After bodywork is repaired, the surface is prepared and refinished so the repaired area sits in with the rest of the vehicle.

  • Paintwork repairs
  • Refinishing after body repairs
  • Restoring a worn or damaged finish

Maintenance and repair

General motor vehicle maintenance and repair, in line with the company's registered activity (SIC 45200, maintenance and repair of motor vehicles). Routine upkeep and repairs that keep a vehicle running as it should.

  • General vehicle maintenance
  • Mechanical repairs
  • Wear-and-tear repairs
